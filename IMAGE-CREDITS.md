# Image credits

## Photography — Flow Forward Africa's own

Every photograph on this site is **Flow Forward Africa's own**, supplied by the
organisation. They show a real menstrual health workshop: girls in the session,
the Flow Forward Africa materials being handed out and read, facilitators
leading the discussion, and the written reflections collected at the end.

No attribution is required and no third-party licence applies. This replaced an
earlier set of Creative Commons stock images, which also removed the CC BY-SA
credit lines that used to sit on the Kilimanjaro carousel.

## Mount Kilimanjaro

The climb has not happened yet, so the mountain is shown with a stock
photograph, hosted on Flow Forward Africa's ImageKit account:

`https://ik.imagekit.io/qcvroy8xpd/stephan-bechert-1ZfMAnL4ubE-unsplash.jpg`

Photograph by **Stephan Bechert** via [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/photos/1ZfMAnL4ubE)
([Unsplash Licence](https://unsplash.com/license) — free for commercial use, no
attribution required). Used everywhere the mountain itself appears, via
`kilimanjaroImage(width, height)` in `lib/site.js`. The source is portrait, so
every crop uses `fo-top` or the mountain falls out of frame.

Replace it with the team's own summit photographs after September.
